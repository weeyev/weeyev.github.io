Hi, I am weeye (pronounced as we+eye, which are the first two letters of my name!), I am currently an undergraduate student with my main research interests in geometric and topological deep learning, my work over at Lossfunk has been involved with artificial life and NCAs, specifically on how we can extract deep insights from simple life forms to build upon our current understanding of consciousness.

More recently I have been interested in computational neuroscience and dynamical systems with the (current) overall goal of understanding memory systems and forgetting dynamics of neuronal circuits

I mostly use this site to archive links that I find interesting on the internet are worth going through, I love reading and lately writing, both of these which you can access here over the due course of time, if any of the above feel interesting to you, I would love to have a chat anytime on Twitter or you can drop a mail at theweeye at proton dot me
